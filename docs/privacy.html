Privacy policy

Wordeck · Last updated 13 October 2026

In short

Wordeck does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What the app stores

Your profiles, settings and study progress are saved only in the app's own storage on your device. They never leave it, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Internet

The app works fully offline and does not connect to the internet. Links on the About page, such as this policy, open in your browser only when you tap them.

Pronunciation

Words are spoken by your device's own text-to-speech voices. The app does not send the words anywhere itself; the speech service of your device (for example from Apple, Google, Samsung or Microsoft) is covered by that provider's privacy policy.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
